FAQ

Questions, answered

Which email providers are supported?
Gmail via official OAuth API, plus any provider with SMTP + IMAP (Zoho, Outlook, cPanel mail, etc.). IMAP IDLE gives near real-time detection on IMAP accounts.
Will the AI invent answers about my business?
No. Replies are grounded in your Knowledge Base via RAG. The model is explicitly instructed never to invent prices or policies — and if no matching knowledge exists, the email is ignored instead of guessed.
What's the difference between Send and Draft mode?
Send replies automatically (with quota reserve → commit checks). Draft prepares the reply in your mailbox for one-click review. Many teams start in Draft, then switch to Send.
How does billing and usage tracking work?
Every auto-send goes through a ledger: the quota is reserved, the mail is sent, then usage is committed. If limits are hit, the send is safely blocked — no surprise overages.
Is my knowledge base data isolated?
Yes. All KB chunks are stored with a tenant ID scoped to your user/account, so no other customer's data can ever surface in your replies.
Can I get notified outside of email?
Absolutely — connect a Telegram bot or WhatsApp Cloud API and receive instant notifications (Pro and above), including chat-assistant access on Telegram.
